ENiShare

Connect to your PC

Enter the PIN

Find the 6-digit pairing PIN in ENiShare on your PC (Settings → Security).

Scan the QR code

  1. On your PC, open ENiShare and click Start Sharing.
  2. Open the iPhone Camera and point it at the QR code on the PC screen.
  3. Tap the link that appears. You are connected — no app needed.

Each QR code works once. If it expired, press Refresh QR on the PC.
